This area covers free and open-source software and the legal rules that programmers meet. It holds a card for each SPDX licence, and pages on how open-source projects work and how to contribute.

## What it holds

- Free and open-source software: the ideas and the history of free software and open source.
- Licences: software licences, with a card for each SPDX licence.
- Contribution practice: how to contribute to a project, and how a project accepts work.
- Governance and foundations: how open-source projects make decisions, and the foundations that host them.
- Copyright and patents for programmers: copyright and patents as a programmer meets them.
- Export rules: export rules for software, at the level of an overview.

## What it does not hold

- Legal advice for a case. The pages explain the rules. They are not legal advice.
- Supply-chain security of dependencies. It is in [Secure programming](/security/).
- Package managers. They are in [Tools and workflow](/tools-and-workflow/).
