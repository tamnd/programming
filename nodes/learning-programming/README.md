This area covers how people learn to program. It holds the research on learning, curricula, the common misconceptions of learners and the places to practise. Each misconception has its own page, linked to the concept that it is about.

## What it holds

- How people learn to program: what research says about how people learn to program.
- Curricula: CS2023, the K-12 frameworks and other curricula.
- Common misconceptions: the common mistaken models of learners, each linked to its concept.
- Exercises and practice sites: exercise and practice sites.
- Notional machines: simple models of the machine for teaching.

## What it does not hold

- The concepts themselves. Each concept has its page in its own area.
- Teaching languages as languages. They are in [Domain programming](/domain-programming/) and under [Languages](/languages/).
- General education theory. This site links out for it.
