This area covers programming for a special field. Each sub-area holds the languages, tools and pitfalls that a programmer meets in that field.

## What it holds

- **Spreadsheets and formula languages.** Spreadsheet tools, their formula languages and the errors of spreadsheet programs.
- **Financial programming.** The rules and formats of financial software, beyond the money type.
- **Bioinformatics programming.** Programming for biology: the formats, libraries, tools and workflow systems of genomics and related fields.
- **Healthcare data.** The data standards and rules of software for health care.
- **Geospatial programming.** Programs that work with places: coordinates, projections, spatial formats and maps.
- **Smart contracts.** Programs that run on a blockchain, their languages and tools, and their failures.
- **Robotics.** Robot programming: ROS, simulators, frames, motion and the languages of industrial robots.
- **Automation and scripting.** Scripts and tools that automate daily work on files, desktops and office programs.
- **Low-code and no-code.** Platforms that build applications and workflows with little or no code, and their limits.
- **Education languages.** Languages and environments made for teaching programming.
- **Creative coding.** Code for art, music and interactive work.

## What it does not hold

- The science of each field. It is out of scope.
- Data science. It is in [Data science and ML programming](/data-science-and-ml-programming/).
- Shell scripting as a language. It has a guide under [Languages](/languages/).
