This area is about how teams plan, build, release and maintain software. It covers requirements, estimation, methods, releases, documentation and the known laws of software projects.

## What it holds

- Requirements in practice: finding out and writing down what a system must do.
- Estimation: estimating the size and the time of work.
- Methodologies: Agile, Scrum, Kanban, XP, waterfall and their practices.
- Releases and versioning: release processes and version numbers, such as semantic versioning.
- Maintenance: keeping software working after its first release.
- Engineering metrics: measures of a team and its output, and their limits.
- Documentation: project documentation, such as design documents and READMEs.
- Team practice: how engineering teams work together.
- Laws of software: Brooks's law, Conway's law, Hyrum's law and others.
- Ethics and professional practice: ethics and professional practice of programmers.

## What it does not hold

- Code-level practice, such as code review. It is in [Construction practice](/construction-practice/).
- CI and CD tools. They are in [Tools and workflow](/tools-and-workflow/).
- Licences and open-source governance. They are in [Open source and licensing](/open-source-and-licensing/).
- Business management that has no link to software. It is out of scope.
